# Elsenburg Enterprise Research — Cloudskraal Farming Area

**Location:** -31.32, 19.02 (Nieuwoudtville / Bokkeveld, Hantam, Northern Cape)
**Biome:** Cape Fynbos / Succulent Karoo | Rainfall: 250-400mm (winter) | Soil: Stony-sand, pH ~6

## 1. Rooibos
- **Yield benchmark:** 300-450 kg/ha (best: 460-470 kg/ha)
- **Soil pH target:** 4.2-4.7 (KCl)
- **Price (2024):** R25-R45/kg farmgate; international R80-250/kg
- **Lifespan:** 4-5 years per planting, then 1-2yr rotation
- **Pests:** Clear-wing moth, Leafhopper, Looper
- **Calendar:** Sow Feb-Mar, Transplant May-Jul, Harvest Dec-Apr
- **SA production:** ~14,000t (2024), declining. Exports 9,700t to 50+ countries

## 2. Sheep (Karoo)
- **Stocking rate norm:** 2.6 ha/SSU (Nama Karoo)
- **Wool price:** R249-313/kg clean (superfine premium)
- **Calendar:** Mating Feb-Apr, Lambing Jul-Sep, Shearing Sep-Oct
- **Key risk:** Drought frequency increasing
- **Elsenburg budgets:** Dohne-Merino, Dorper, SA Merino available

## 3. Wine Grapes (Olifants River)
- **Yield benchmark:** 25t/ha (high for SA)
- **Production cost:** ~R35,000/ha
- **Replanting cost:** ~R225,000/ha (every 25yr)
- **Net margin:** Very tight for bulk wine
- **Cultivars:** Chenin Blanc, Sauvignon Blanc, Colombard

## 4. Buchu
- **Oil yield:** 1-2% of fresh material
- **Oil price:** ~USD 850/kg
- **Soil pH:** 4.0-4.99 optimal
- **Challenge:** Low germination, poor transplant survival (<10%)

## 5. Sceletium
- **NEMBA permit REQUIRED** for any commercialisation
- **Benefit-sharing agreement** needed with Khoi-San communities
- **Propagation:** Cuttings preferred, 80% success rate
- **Dormant in summer** — do not water
