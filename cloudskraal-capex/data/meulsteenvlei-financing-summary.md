# Meulsteenvlei R12M Purchase — Financing Model Summary

**Date:** 25 March 2026
**Target:** R13,120,000 by Q4 2028 (R12M purchase + R1.12M transfer duty)
**Status:** Agreement signed, occupation taken October 2025, lease R1.1M/year until purchase

---

## Executive Summary

The Meulsteenvlei purchase is fundable through a combination of farm operating cash flow, director loan earmarking, BRRRR property equity, and an agricultural bank loan. The base case achieves 99.9% coverage of the R13.12M target. With a modest vendor financing negotiation or slightly larger bank facility, the deal is comfortably covered with margin.

**Post-purchase, the asset immediately generates R2.1M/year** (R1.1M lease saving + R1M Elandsvoetpad tea), providing 2.2x debt service coverage on a R7M bank loan.

## Conclusion

The Meulsteenvlei purchase is achievable with disciplined cash flow management and a moderate agricultural bank loan. The funding model shows:

1. **R6.1M own resources** (farm FCF + director loans + BRRRR) covers 47% of the purchase
2. **R7M bank loan** covers the remaining 53%, well within Land Bank's 80% LTV limit
3. **Post-purchase cash flows** of R2.1M/year service the bank debt at 2.2x coverage
4. **The asset itself generates R6.16M/year** in total value — exceptional ROI

The biggest risk is rooibos price volatility. Mitigation: maintain flexibility to increase the bank facility up to R9.6M if needed. Start the Land Bank pre-approval process by Q4 2027 to ensure financing is available when needed.
