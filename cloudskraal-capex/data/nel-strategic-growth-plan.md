## 1. Executive Summary

Cloudskraal Boerderye is a seven-generation rooibos farming operation in the Nieuwoudtville district, Northern Cape. Revenue has grown from R4.3M (2023) to R10.1M (2025), driven by rooibos price appreciation and volume growth. The operation spans 1,049 ha across four farms with 53 fields, producing approximately 232 tonnes of rooibos annually at R40/kg.

Despite strong revenue growth, the farm operates at a slight accounting loss. The real profit engine is R2.3M/year in interest income from R25.5M in director loans — a structure that creates significant estate duty exposure and must be unwound over the next decade.

The critical strategic move is the R12M Meulsteenvlei purchase (agreement signed, due Q4 2028). This property holds all processing infrastructure, 200 ha of tea plantings, and 6,000 m2 of warehousing. Without Meulsteenvlei, Cloudskraal cannot control its processing. With it, the operation gains R2.1M/year in immediate value (R1.1M lease saving + R1M Elandsvoetpad tea) and unlocks direct export capability.

**The plan in one paragraph:** Restructure the entity group for tax efficiency and succession protection (2026-2027). Acquire Meulsteenvlei (2028). Build processing and export capability through Alexenya (2028-2030). Launch own-brand premium rooibos exports and tourism income streams (2030-2033). Consolidate a diversified R30-50M operation with mature governance, fully retired founders, and the next generation firmly in control (2034-2036).

**How we fund it:** Farm operating cash flow (R4.4M cumulative by 2028), R1.5M director loan earmarking, R7M agricultural bank loan, and modest BRRRR property equity. Post-Meulsteenvlei, the asset itself generates 2.2x debt service coverage. No outside equity is needed. No family assets are at risk beyond what is already committed.

### 5.3 Crop Diversification — Buchu & Sceletium

The Bokkeveld has ideal conditions for both species. They command premium prices and are in growing demand.

**Buchu (Agathosma betulina):**
- Wild harvest + controlled cultivation
- Essential oil and dried leaf markets
- R200-800/kg depending on grade and processing
- Low input crop, compatible with existing rooibos rotations
- Start: 5-10 ha trial plots in 2031

**Sceletium (Sceletium tortuosum — "kanna"):**
- Rapidly growing nutraceutical market (mood, anxiety, cognitive function)
- R500-2,000/kg for dried material
- **CRITICAL:** NEMBA (National Environmental Management: Biodiversity Act) requires permits for commercial cultivation and bioprospecting compliance under ABS (Access and Benefit Sharing) regulations. Legal costs: R50-100K. Timeline: 12-18 months for permits.
- Start: feasibility study and legal compliance in 2030, first planting 2032

### Key Observations

1. **Revenue crosses R20M in 2029** (Meulsteenvlei acquisition effect) and **R30M by 2032**.
2. **The export premium is transformative.** At 50% direct export by 2034, rooibos alone generates R29M+ vs R13.5M on pure bulk pricing. This is the single biggest value lever in the plan.
3. **EBITDA exceeds R10M from 2029.** The business becomes genuinely profitable (not just loan-income profitable) from the moment Meulsteenvlei is acquired.
4. **Cumulative equity reaches R91M by 2036.** This is the total retained earnings over the decade — the actual wealth created by the farming operation.
5. **Director loan interest phases out by 2034** as loans are repaid. By then, operational profitability more than compensates.
6. **The 2029 dip in net income** is the Meulsteenvlei acquisition year — heavy CapEx and new debt service temporarily compress margins. This is expected and manageable.

| Scenario | Impact on 2036 Revenue | Impact on 2036 Net Income |
|----------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| Rooibos price grows 5%/yr instead of 8% | R32.5M (vs R42.3M) | R9.2M (vs R12.8M) |

| Drought: 20% production loss for 2 years (2030-2031) | R38.9M (recovers) | R10.8M (by 2036) |
| All three adverse scenarios combined | R22.4M | R5.1M — still profitable, still viable |

Even in the worst combined scenario, the business generates positive net income throughout. The downside is slower growth, not existential risk.
