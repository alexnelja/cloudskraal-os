## Read-through for the COP / scenario model

- **2026's R31.65 sits almost exactly on the 30-year real average** — the current price
  regime is mid-cycle, neither boom nor bust.
- **Cycle amplitude is brutal:** trough R8.73 (2010), peak R86.73 (2018) — a 10× range in
  real terms. The 2016–2019 export boom (R62–87) and the 2020–2022 oversupply slump
  (R18–27) bracket the realistic planning envelope.
- Booms run ~4 years (2015–2019), slumps ~3 (2008–2011, 2020–2022); recovery legs
  (2012–2015, 2023–2026) grind back to mean over ~4 years.
- The internal 2027–2030 forecast (R46→R55→R45→R39 on the **netto** basis ≈ R37→R44→R36→R31
  blended at the 2026 fraction mix) implies a modest above-mean swing, nothing like 2018 —
  defensible against this history.
- For what-if/scenario tooling (2h node map; price is THE master lever per
  `rooibos-cost-structure-2026-06.md`): sensible price scenarios on the blended basis are
  **bear R18 / base R31 / bull R55+** from this distribution.
- Breakeven context: Cloudskraal COP straddles break-even in the R39–55 netto band — at
  trough prices (R9–18 blended) nobody covers cost; the surplus-pool mechanism
  (~196k kg stored, see `rooibos-ltd-2026-purchase-agreement.md`) is the price-cycle
  arbitrage instrument.
