# Rooibos buyers — quality metrics, sifts and pricing

Reference for allocating the Cloudskraal rooibos crop between buyers. Built from the 2026 season documents (sources at the end). Update annually when the prysaankondigings and Aankoopsooreenkoms arrive.

**Our baseline:** teehof sif set 10 → 40 maas. Independently measured snit on our 2026 export-quality tea (Cape Natural lab, 400 g sample, 27 May 2026): 2.75% stok (>10), 11.75% in the 10–12 band, 84.5% in 12–40, 1.0% dust. **96.25% of mass between 10 and 40 maas.**

---

## Sieve (maas) basics

Higher maas number = finer particle. "Sieving >12" = passes the 10 sieve, retained on the 12 sieve.

| Band | Common name | Rooibos Bpk term | Cape Natural term |
|---|---|---|---|
| >10 | Sticks | Stok | Sieving >10 |
| 10–12 | Coarse | Grof | Sieving >12 |
| 12–18 | Coarse tea | (part of tee, band starts at 16) | Sieving >18 (faktor 0.90) |
| 18–20 | Tea | Tee | Sieving >20 (faktor 1.00) |
| 20–40 | Fine tea | Tee | Sieving >40 (faktor 1.00, earns premie) |
| <40 | Dust | Stof | Dust <40 (faktor 0.25) |

**Key mismatch:** our teehof counts everything 10–40 as netto tea (~95–96%). Both buyers' full-value tea band is narrower (Rooibos Bpk from 16 maas; Cape Natural full value from 18/20 maas). Farm netto ≠ buyer tee%. Expect buyer tea/value yield of 78–84% on our tea, not 95%.

## Buyer 1: Rooibos Bpk (Clanwilliam) — CLO010

### Model: fixed fraction pricing

Each load priced as four mass fractions × fraction price, plus quality adjustments. 2026 grade basis: **D06R1P2**, average ~R31.65/kg.

| Fraksie | 2026 prys/kg | Applied % |
|---|---|---|
| Stok (>10) | R3.50 | **8.00 fixed/floor** |
| Grof (>12) | R5.60 | **12.00 floor** (measured if worse, e.g. Kromvlei 13.4) |
| Tee (16–40) | R39.60 | Remainder (our loads: 67–79%) |
| Stof (<40) | R20.00 | Measured (our loads: 1.3–12.7%) |

Per a 2021 producer newsletter, the 8% stok / 12% grof has been applied as a fixed rule since 2022. Not in the signed Aankoopsooreenkoms (appears only as "maasverdeling aanname" in the clause 4 price illustration). Consequence: **no credit for pre-sieving below 8/12; penalty when worse.** Our measured 2.75% stok vs the fixed 8% costs ~R1.90/kg (~R280 000 on the 2026 season's 148 819 kg).

### Quality adjustments (added/deducted per kg)

- **Sensories (cup):** 6 points = basis; +R1 per point up to +R6 at 12; −R1 to −R3 at 5–3; 1–2 unacceptable. Our 2026 loads: 6–7.
- **Residu (MRL list):** R1 (not detected) = basis; R2 −R1; R3 −R3; R4 −R5. We score R1 consistently.
- **PA-vlakke:** P1 (≤0.05) +R1; P2 (0.05–0.2) = basis; P3 −R1; P4 −R2; P5 −R3. We score P1–P2.

### Deductions

**Verwerkingskoste**, banded on stok%, charged on full bruto kg: 0–11.9% → R4.00/kg; 12–15.9% → R4.40; 16%+ → R5.80. We sit in the R4.00 band (fixed 8%).

### Payment & structure

- Voorskot R18.00/kg ~15th of month after delivery; finale betaling 23 September.
- **Quota system (VVS):** 2026 VVS 92 432 kg; intake offer 120 000 kg vs estimated crop 178 000 kg.
- Overflow → **B-poel** (counts as next year's Hoofpoel, capped at Hoofpoel contracted qty) or **Surpluspoel** (stored ≤10 yr, our asset, self-insured, sold only when they offer at the then Hoofpoel price; declining triggers backdated 8c/kg/month storage). In storage: 78 648 kg (2024) + 117 265 kg (2025) = **195 913 kg, ~R6m unrealised**.
- Organic/RA/Fairtrade premiums paid only on volumes they onsell as certified (clause 7.4).

### Effective 2026 net on our tea

~R32.26 gross − R4.00 verwerking = **~R28.26/kg**, paid in two legs ending September.

## Buyer 2: Cape Natural Tea Products (Vanrhynsdorp) — V-CLO001

### Model: waardemodel (measured value yield)

Per-load 400 g sample, measured snit, value factor per fraction. Betaling = waarde-opbrengs % × basisprys + grade premie op fyn fraksies. Percentages are **not shown on the invoice** — request the Afleweringsbewys per load.

| Fraksie | Faktor | Effective value (× R40.45) |
|---|---|---|
| >10 | 0.18 | ~R7.28 |
| >12 (10–12) | 0.22 | ~R8.90 |
| >18 (12–18) | 0.90 | ~R36.41 |
| >20 | 1.00 | R40.45 |
| >40 (20–40) | 1.00 | R40.45 |
| Dust <40 | 0.25 | ~R10.11 |

### 2026 prices (prysaankondigings, May 2026)

- **Konvensioneel:** basis R40.45/kg (ex VAT, before waardemodel); their stated average after waardemodel ~R31.95/kg (+6% on 2025).
- **RA konvensioneel:** basis R45.10/kg (~11% certification premium in the base).
- **Premies (pro rata on fine fractions):** Uitvoergraad R8.00/kg op 20-en-40 maas fraksie; Versnitgraad R4.00 op 20-en-40; Uitvoer-en-versnitgraad R4.00 op 18 maas; Plaaslike graad geen. *(Exact band wording ambiguous — confirm.)*
- Sensory scored per load (cup aroma/colour/taste, leaf shade) — drives grade classification rather than direct R adjustments.

### Payment & structure

- Full invoicing of price differences in June — **~3 months earlier than Rooibos Bpk's final**.
- No explicit verwerkingskoste (embedded in factors/base).
- No quota/pool system evident; volume appetite **unconfirmed** — to negotiate.

### Effective 2026 net on our tea (load MAT-0379, 24 700 kg)

Waarde-opbrengs 83.69% × R40.45 = **R33.85/kg** base; + uitvoer premie (43% fine fraction × R8.00) ≈ R3.44 → **up to ~R37.29/kg**, paid June.

## Head-to-head on our 2026 export-quality tea

| | Rooibos Bpk | Cape Natural |
|---|---|---|
| Snit | Fixed 8/12 floor | Measured per load |
| Effective net/kg | ~R28.26 | ~R33.85–37.29 |
| Per 30 t load | ~R848 000 | ~R1 016 000–1 119 000 |
| Settlement | R18 advance + Sep final | ~June, full |
| Pre-sieving rewarded | No (floor) | Yes (factors + premies) |
| Dust tolerance | Better (stof R20.00) | Worse (factor 0.25 ≈ R10) |
| Certification premium | Only if onsold as certified | RA in base price (R45.10) |
| Over-quota tonnes | Pool: deferred/unpriced | Cash |

Gap: **R5.60–9.00/kg (~20–32%)** in Cape Natural's favour on clean tea, before counting the pool effect.

## Allocation logic for coming years

1. **The binding constraint is the Rooibos Bpk quota, not price.** At ~200 t production vs ~120 t intake, ±80 t/yr has no cash home at Rooibos Bpk — it accumulates in pools (now ~196 t). Marginal tonnes to Cape Natural earn ~R1.7–1.9m per 50 t *in the year of harvest* vs deferred, unpriced pool entries.
2. **Match tea prep to buyer model.** Cleanest, finest, pre-sieved export tea → Cape Natural (measurement + premies reward it; fine 20–40 fraction earns R8). Rougher tea → Rooibos Bpk (the 8/12 floor is charged regardless; their stof price is double CN's, so dustier loads hurt less there). Avoid over-fine cutting for CN (dust factor 0.25) — Elandsvoetpad-type loads (12.7% stof) are better placed at Rooibos Bpk.
3. **Pre-sieving for Rooibos Bpk deliveries is value-negative under the fixed rule** — we pay sieving costs, deliver fewer kg, and get docked 8% anyway. Either negotiate measured snit for pre-sieved loads or deliver closer to bruto (keeping stok <11.9% for the R4.00 verwerking band).
4. **Certification:** if RA/organic volumes grow, Cape Natural pays the premium in the base price; Rooibos Bpk only passes it through if onsold as certified.
5. **Relationship/risk balance:** ~196 t of our capital sits in Rooibos Bpk's store under their terms — keep the relationship functional and confirm insurance cover on pool stock. Sequence: secure CN volume commitment → then negotiate Rooibos grading/quota.
6. **Cash flow:** every tonne shifted to CN also pulls settlement forward ~3 months.
