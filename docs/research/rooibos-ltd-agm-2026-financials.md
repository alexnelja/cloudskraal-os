**Read-through for Cloudskraal:**

1. **Counterparty strength is excellent** — fortress balance sheet (current ratio 13:1,
   nearly no debt, R1.4bn cash+portfolio). The ~196 t / ~R6m Cloudskraal surplus-pool tea
   stored at their risk profile is safe on solvency grounds (insurance still our problem).
2. **The inventory mountain explains the quota.** Stock up R117m to R387m = 407 days of
   sales; chairman: industry holds large reserves, Rooibos Bpk deliberately building a
   "reserwevoorraad". This is WHY the VVS quota (~120 t vs our ~178 t crop) and the
   surplus-pool mechanism exist — they are long tea. Don't expect intake generosity in
   2027; the Cape Natural channel matters more, not less.
3. **Shareholder economics vs producer economics.** EPS R372 vs R30 dividend = ~8%
   payout; the rest compounds in the share portfolio (R1.1bn, +R201m gains in 2025).
   The producer-shareholder gets paid mainly through the share, not the tea price.
4. **Governance flags (qualified opinion, 3 grounds + 1 company-level):** associates
   (~R16.5m Naturally Out of Africa NL/SA + Rooibos Asia Pacific — ceased operations)
   unverifiable; PP&E useful lives not reassessed; **R24.1m inventory not stock-count
   verified**; Maluti Rosehip loan recoverability (R21.6m company-level). Prior-year US
   subsidiary (Rooibos North America, rev R70.0m) was **unaudited by directors' election**.
   Same qualifications repeated from 2024 — worth a question from the floor.
5. Subsidiaries FY2025: Extracts R16.4m · Marketing Europe R13.0m · Marketing SA
   −R3.9m · Honeybush R0.6m · Maluti Rosehip R10.3m (2024: −R15.7m) · RNA R3.9m.
   Related-company loans: Maluti R43.2m (down from R62.0m), Oribi Mountain R18.7m.

## Rooibos Beleggings (Edms) Bpk FY2025 (audited, CLEAN opinion)

Producers' investment vehicle (2012: R110m of Rooibos Bpk retained earnings spun into
an equity-portfolio holding; shares allocated pro rata to Rooibos Bpk shareholding).

- Total assets **R346.4m** (portfolio R344.4m) · equity **R301.5m** · deferred tax R44.8m.
- FY2025 profit R51.5m (R61.2m fair-value gains − costs/tax); dividends paid only R153k —
  it compounds, it doesn't distribute.
- **Liquidity = the buy-back window:** annually up to 5% of issued shares may be sold
  back. FY2025: 108,880 shares (1.34% of 8,122,961) bought back; 2024 price R34.26/share
  vs **NAV ≈ R37–38/share** (equity ÷ ~8.0m shares) — buy-back runs slightly below NAV.
- Directors: Hanekom (chair), M G Bergh (MD — **45.26% indirect interest**), Joubert, Kuiper.

**Read-through:** Cloudskraal's Beleggings shares are a quiet compounding asset with one
exit door per year (the 5% window, priced near NAV). If capital is ever needed for the
farm (e.g. Meulsteenvlei), the buy-back window is the mechanism and its timing matters.
