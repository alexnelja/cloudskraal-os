## Price Estimation Algorithm

For commodities without free real-time price feeds:

```
estimated_price = index_price × grade_adjustment × location_factor × freshness_discount

where:
- index_price = latest available index (World Bank, LME, LBMA, or platform avg)
- grade_adjustment = (actual_grade / index_grade) × value_in_use_factor
  - e.g., chrome 44% vs index 42%: 44/42 × 1.0 = 1.048
- location_factor = 1.0 for FOB SA ports, varies for CIF:
  - CIF China ≈ FOB + freight_cost / price
  - freight_cost from Haversine distance × $/nm/t factor
- freshness_discount = max(0.95, 1 - days_since_index × 0.001)
  - 1% discount per 10 days of stale data
```
