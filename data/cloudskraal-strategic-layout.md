**Key trends:**
1. **Revenue collapsed** from R14.4M (2020) to R3.2M (2023) — a 78% decline over 3 years. Likely driven by drought and rooibos price cycles. Recovery to R6.7M in 2025.
2. **Operating expenses peaked** at R12.5M (2021) then were cut to R6.7M (2024) — a 47% cost reduction. Crept back to R7.3M in 2025 driven by R1.8M repairs/maintenance.
3. **Farm operates at a loss** in 5 of 6 years at the operating level. Only 2020 was profitable from farming alone (R4.9M operating profit on R14.4M revenue).
4. **Interest income is the profit engine** — consistently R2.2-2.7M/year from loans to JAL Nel and F Nel. This saved the business during 2022-2023 losses.
5. **2022-2023 were crisis years** — combined losses of R4.1M. Revenue halved while costs stayed high.
6. **2024-2025 is the recovery** — revenue doubled from trough, costs controlled, back to profitability.
7. **Tax:** R1.96M paid in 2020, R709K in 2021, nothing 2022-2024 (carried forward losses), R76K in 2025 — the losses created a significant tax shield.

## 4. Production Systems

### 4.1 Rooibos (Primary Revenue Driver)

**Scale:** Multiple fields across Cloudskraal, Garsland, and leased land. Horizontal rooibos potential reached — future growth through leased land, yield improvement, and processing quality.

**Production Flow:**
1. **Field harvest** (hand-cut with teabags, or machine) → Transport to factory
2. **Machine cutting** (Bovic/Victor cutters) → Tea bins
3. **Overnight oxidation heaps** (monitor 37-39°C) → Morning opening
4. **Tea court drying** (weather dependent)
5. **Bagging** (unsifted) → Sieving → Grading
6. **Final storage** → Sale to off-takers

**Season:** January to mid-April (daily cycle during harvest).

**Key bottlenecks identified:**
- Tea court capacity limits daily throughput (expansion is a capex priority)
- Teabag design (Jan van Niekerk) — people wait on field for bags
- Blade sharpness on cutters affects throughput
- Factory layout needs one-way flow optimization

**Processing facilities:**
- **Cloudskraal:** 2x Bovic cutters, 2x Victor cutters, tea court, storage
- **Garsland factory:** 3x Bovic cutters, factory occupied from Nov 2025

**KPIs tracked:**
- Total rooibos produced (tonnes)
- Grade/quality per day of drying
- Total cost of production per kg (field inputs + harvest + processing)
- Yield per field

## 9. Regional Context

**Climate:** Semi-arid Mediterranean. 350-400mm rainfall (winter-dominant). Frost common Jun-Aug. Summer maxima 35°C+. Highly variable — drought years <200mm.

**Soils:** Bokkeveld shale-derived (clay-rich), sandy pockets suitable for rooibos and buchu. Dolerite intrusions create richer red soils. Dorbank hardpan in places.

**Water:** Water-stressed region. Boreholes are primary irrigation source (yields vary 1-12 L/s). Oorlogskloof River and seasonal streams. Strict Department of Water and Sanitation allocations.

**Tourism:** Nieuwoudtville is the "Bulb Capital of the World." Hantam National Botanical Garden (SANBI). Aug-Sep flower season is the anchor event. Limited accommodation = opportunity.

**Comparable operations:**
- **Babylonstoren** (Franschhoek): 200ha+, R200M+ turnover, 500+ staff. Anchor: edible garden + luxury accommodation.
- **Boschendal** (Franschhoek): Heritage narrative (300+ years), farm-to-table, wine club, luxury cottages.
- **Cederberg Private Cellar** (~1,000m): Proved altitude SA wine commands premiums.

**Key difference for Cloudskraal:** Unlike Winelands estates, Cloudskraal is remote. The wildflower season is the anchor draw, not wine route traffic. This means accommodation must be compelling enough to justify the journey — and shoulder-season experiences (rooibos harvest Jan-Apr, sheep, stargazing, hiking) must fill gaps.

## 15. Meulsteenvlei Financing Strategy — Boland Property Leverage

**The problem:** R12M due end 2028. Current net profit ~R1.3M/year (growing). Farm cash flow alone won't accumulate R12M in 2.5 years.

**The strategy:** Buy undervalued Boland properties → rehab → revalue → refinance at higher LTV → use extracted equity/overdraft facility to fund R12M.

### How it works

```
Year 1 (2026):  Buy Property A → R4M (80% LTV = R3.2M debt, R800K equity)
                → Rehab R800K
                → Total in: R4.8M
                → Revalue at R6.5-7M (135-145% of cost)
                → Refinance at 70% of new value = R4.55-4.9M facility
                → Equity extracted: ~R1.5-1.9M above original debt
                → Rental income services debt in the meantime

Year 2 (2027):  Buy Property B with extracted equity
                → Repeat cycle
                → Accumulate additional facility

End 2028:       Combined facility + farm cash + family loan structure
                → Fund R12M Meulsteenvlei purchase
```
