A good maintenance management approach includes:

- An inventory of all equipment and vehicles, with details like model, purchase date, service intervals, and responsible person.
- A schedule of preventive maintenance (e.g., tractor service every 250 hours or annually, irrigation pump inspection every 6 months, etc.).
- A way to log issues and repairs performed, building a **maintenance history** for each asset. This history helps in decisions like when to replace a machine, or diagnosing recurring problems.
