# Notes for the Farm

This framework breaks down systems into five key lenses:

1. **System Purpose & Boundaries**
2. **Structure & Elements**
3. **Processes & Flows**
4. **Feedback Loops**
5. **Leverage Points for Intervention / Improvement**

---

## **🧭 1. System Purpose & Boundaries**

**Overall Purpose:**

To operate an integrated agricultural system that optimizes **rooibos tea production**, **sheep farming**, **vineyards**, and **lucerne irrigation**, while steadily transitioning the new farm into Cloudskraal’s broader operations — ultimately improving economic viability, resilience, and quality.

**Key Boundaries:**

- **Spatial:** Nieuwoudtville & Willemsrivier region; new farm site + Cloudskraal estate; factory site; rooibos fields; sheep grazing camps; vineyards (10 ha); lucerne pivot system; neighboring radio mast.
- **Temporal:** Immediate (Oct 2025 – Jan 2026 factory commissioning), annual operational cycles for rooibos (Jan–Apr harvest), sheep (Dec joining / May lambing), vines (Aug–Mar), and lucerne.
- **Regulatory & contractual:**
    - Rooibos processing under SA agricultural regulations.
    - Tenant field lease runs until end 2028 → **fields not accessible** until then; factory can be used immediately.
    - Wool logistics with KLK.
    - Potential ICASA licensing for radio repeater.

## **🔄 3. Processes & Flows**

### **A. Rooibos Flow**

Field harvest → Transport → Machine cut → Bins → Overnight oxidation heaps → Morning opening (37–39 °C) → Tea court drying → Bagging (unsifted) → Sieving → Grading → Final storage.

*Daily cycle Jan–mid Apr.*

### **B. Sheep Cycle (Annual)**

Pre-breeding prep (Oct–Nov) → Ram joining (mid-Dec, 17 days) → Gestation (150 days) → Lambing (May) → Vaccination cycle (Jul–Aug) → Weaning → Bluetongue vaccination (Aug–Sep) → Next joining (Dec).

### **C. Vines Annual Cycle**

Prune (Aug–Sep) → Budburst (Oct) → Flowering (Nov) → Fruit set (Dec) → Veraison (Jan) → Harvest (Feb–Mar).

### **D. Lucerne**

Pivot irrigation → Soil-based scheduling → Weekly maintenance rounds → Cut for feed or sale.

### **E. Infrastructure Flows**

- **Construction:** sandstone heaps earmarked for building ablution/accommodation.
- **Water:** planned dam near Willemsrivier.
- **Comms:** Radio mast → repeater → handheld/base units across team.

### **F. People & Information Flows**

- Appie ↔ Sheep & workers (daily radios, task calendars).
- Adrie ↔ Pivot & lucerne scheduling.
- Japie & Alex ↔ strategic decisions, logistics, development.
- Radios will knit together daily operational feedback.

## **🔁 4. Feedback Loops**

| **Feedback Loop** | **Type** | **Effect** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rooibos drying feedback** (court temp/moisture vs drying time) | Balancing | Adjusts heap moisture & tarping to stabilize daily throughput. |
| **Sheep fertility & lambing % loop** (nutrition → conception → scanning → selection) | Reinforcing | Better nutrition & ram fitness improves fertility → better scanning → selection improves genetics → fertility improves. |
| **Vaccination & disease loop** | Balancing | Timely vaccination reduces disease incidence → stabilizes flock health → reduces unexpected losses. |
| **Pivot maintenance & lucerne yield** | Balancing | Regular checks keep yield stable; neglected pivots create reinforcing negative loops (low pressure → dry patches → weed invasion). |
| **Radio communication & response** | Reinforcing | Better comms → faster problem detection → faster response → smoother operations. |
| **Factory throughput & blade sharpness** | Reinforcing | Sharp blades → faster cutting → shorter queues → easier maintenance scheduling → keeps blades sharp. |

## **🪄 5. Leverage Points (Improvement Areas)**

### **High-Leverage (Systemic)**

- **Tea court expansion & process control**: Court size and heap management set the **ceiling** for daily rooibos throughput. A modest investment here unlocks full factory efficiency.
- **Vaccination calendar discipline**: Align clostridial boosters and bluetongue strictly with breeding windows to prevent losses and improve lambing percentages.
- **Radio network deployment**: Improves response loops across all subsystems (factory, sheep, pivots, logistics).
- **Factory layout optimization**: One-way flow and clear zones can reduce bottlenecks without extra machines.

### **Medium-Leverage (Supporting)**

- Blade sharpening rota (throughput gains).
- Ram fitness pen pre-joining.
- Simple pivot inspection routine.
- Temperature & moisture logging for heaps (to tune oxidation).

### **Low-Leverage (Tactical)**

- Minor equipment relocations, labeling sandstone heaps, signage, sifting tweaks.
