## 1. Solar PV (70 kWp) + Battery Storage — Farm Processing Facility

**Context:** Grid-tied hybrid system with Eskom for the Garsland processing plant. Load profile: 2x 24 kW cutters + 15 kW sifter = ~63 kW peak. Battery storage sized for 4-6 hours backup at full load (252-378 kWh usable). Northern Cape irradiance is excellent at 5.5-6.5 kWh/m2/day, yielding ~120-140 MWh/year from a 70 kWp array.

**Tax note:** Section 12BA (125% deduction) expired 28 February 2025 and was not renewed. **Section 12B still applies**: 100% first-year write-off for solar PV installations under 1 MW used in trade. The full cost is deductible in the year of commissioning.

## 7. Sceletium Pilot Project — 1-2 Hectares

**Context:** Sceletium tortuosum (kanna/kougoed) is a succulent endemic to the Karoo/Namaqualand region. It contains mesembrine alkaloids with anxiolytic and mood-enhancing properties. Growing commercial demand (Zembrin extract by HG&H Pharmaceuticals). First commercially cultivated in 1996. Propagation by cuttings (preferred) or seed. Harvest at 12-18 months. Requires well-drained sandy soil (available on Cloudskraal), partial shade, and minimal but consistent irrigation.

**Regulatory requirement:** Bioprospecting permit under NEMBA/BABS regulations is mandatory for commercial cultivation and sale. Benefit-sharing agreement with San and Khoikhoi communities required.

## 8. Soil Amendment Program — 500 ha Rooibos Land (3-Year Program)

**Context:** The Bokkeveld rooibos lands have typical fynbos soils: acidic (pH 3.5-5.0), sandy, nutrient-poor, very low organic matter (<1%). Rooibos is adapted to these conditions but responds well to targeted amendments — particularly potassium (K) for plant vigour and stress tolerance, and organic matter for water retention and microbial activity.

**Approach:** Phased 3-year program. Year 1: soil analysis + initial amendments on priority blocks. Years 2-3: expand coverage and apply mulch/compost trials.

### Year 1 — Baseline and Priority Blocks (~150-200 ha)

| Component | LOW/ha | MID/ha | HIGH/ha | Notes |
|-----------|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| Soil analysis (baseline, 1 sample per 5 ha) | R60 | R100 | R150 | Bemlab/SGS; pH, P, K, Ca, Mg, organic C, CEC |
| Potassium application (KCl or K2SO4, 50-100 kg/ha) | R300 | R500 | R750 | K2SO4 preferred for acidic soils (less acidifying) |
| Lime application (agricultural lime, 1-2 t/ha where needed) | R400 | R700 | R1,000 | Only where pH < 4.0; rooibos prefers acid but extreme acidity limits yield |
| Organic matter (compost/kraal manure, 2-5 t/ha) | R600 | R1,200 | R2,000 | Transport is the cost driver; local kraal manure cheapest |
| Spreading/application (mechanical) | R200 | R350 | R500 | Tractor + spreader; rough terrain premium |
| **Year 1 per hectare** | **R1,560** | **R2,850** | **R4,400** | |
